The short version

  • No advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Only a few functional items are stored: theme choice and cached site content.
  • Embedded videos and external links may set their own cookies.
  • You can clear everything at any time in your browser.

01What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage do the same job inside your browser. This site relies on a few of these to work properly and to remember your choices.

This site does not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, analytics trackers or social media tracking scripts.

02What This Site Stores

Everything below stays on your device and is never used to build a profile of you.

03Cookies From Embedded Content

Some content comes from other companies. They may set their own cookies once you load or interact with it:

  • YouTube and Vimeo: videos are embedded and may set cookies when you press play. YouTube embeds use the privacy-enhanced domain.
  • Google Fonts: loading fonts sends a request to Google’s servers, which may log basic technical data.
  • WhatsApp, Calendly, LinkedIn, Fiverr and Upwork: these set cookies on their own sites once you click through to them.

I do not control these cookies. Please see each provider’s policy for details.

04Managing Your Preferences

Because the site does not use advertising or tracking cookies, there is very little to opt out of. You are still in control:

  • Use the theme button at the top of any page to change your saved display preference
  • Clear your browser’s site data to remove everything this site has stored
  • Block third-party cookies in your browser settings if you prefer. The site will still work, although embedded videos may behave differently.

For how personal data is handled more broadly, read the Privacy Policy.

06Changes to This Policy

If I add analytics, advertising or other tools in future, I will update this page first and show a clear notice and a choice where the law requires it. The “Last updated” date shows the current version.
